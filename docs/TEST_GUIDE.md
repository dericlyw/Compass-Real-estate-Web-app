# Tester guide — PropVid Content Engine (test round 1)

**Time:** about 30 minutes. **You need:** the link and the access code from Deric.
**Nothing is live.** No ad is published, no message is sent, and leads you submit are test entries.

Use **✎ Give feedback on this page** (left menu) whenever something breaks, confuses you or is missing. One note per point.

## Before you start (operator only, once)
1. **Settings** → enter the developer licence and APDL numbers. For the test round, values marked `TEST-ONLY` are fine. Without them every piece is blocked, by design.
2. **Content Engine → Brand voice & baseline** → fill in the hours and RM per month currently spent on content. Real figures only.

## Task 1 — Angle (5 min)
1. Open **Content Engine**.
2. Tick 2–3 buyer problems you recognise, or type ones you heard this week.
3. Get angles: Claude suggests them (if switched on), or copy the prompt shown into Claude yourself and add the one you like under **Write an angle yourself**.
4. Try one vague angle on purpose (e.g. "Tips for buying property") and see what happens.
5. Choose one specific angle.

*Tell us:* Were the angles specific enough to post? Did the "too vague" reasons make sense?

## Task 2 — Draft (5 min)
1. On the chosen angle, pick a language, platform and format → **Draft a piece**.
2. Read the draft once in full, then press **I've read it in full**.

*Tell us:* Would you post this draft after light edits? Does the BM / 中文 read as native, not translated?

## Task 3 — Polish (10 min)
1. Look at the generic-phrase flags. Fix them in the text, or keep one with **Override** and a reason.
2. Add one real detail only TKB would know. It must appear in the post text.
3. **Save & re-check**, then **Mark polished**.

*Tell us:* Were the flags right? Anything flagged that is fine in Malaysian property copy? Anything generic that it missed?

## Task 4 — Repeat (5 min)
1. Back on **Content Engine** → **Schedule polished pieces for next week**.
2. Open **Approval Queue** → the piece has a gold "Content Engine" tag → approve or reject it with a comment.
3. **Launch Kit** → download Meta → open `ads.csv` and find your piece.
4. Optional: download the weekly calendar invite (.ics).

*Tell us:* Would you sit this session every Monday? What would stop you?

## Task 5 — Overall (2 min)
Leave one final note on the **Feedback** page with a usefulness score from 1 to 5.

---

### For Deric — reading the results
- **Feedback received** in the menu lists every note. **Download CSV** opens in Excel/Sheets.
- The **Audit Log** shows who did what, and when.
- **Scorecard** on Content Engine: minutes per stage, flags per piece, override rate. A high override rate means the phrase lists need tuning.
