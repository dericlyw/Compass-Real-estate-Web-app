# Campaign Brief — Urban Forest @ Bercham (pilot)

**Objective:** booked sales-gallery visits, measured to SPA. Views are diagnostics, not goals.
**Source film:** https://youtu.be/BK0CO1hdOqA (3:27, labelled "Made with AI" on YouTube — all visuals carry *Artist's impression*).
**Languages:** English, Bahasa Malaysia, Simplified Chinese — written natively, not translated.

## What the film gives us
| Strongest moments | Why they work |
|---|---|
| "When the day's journey comes to an end, where do visitors go?" (~0:12) | Open question, high stopping power |
| "In Ipoh, no experience is complete without food." (~0:55) | Local pride; food is Ipoh's identity |
| "We don't wait until completion and hope it works." (~1:38) | Contrarian statement; speaks to investor fear |
| "128 eateries curated without duplication" (~2:52) | Specific, memorable number (pending brochure check) |

**Held back:** "5.5% return, long-term lease, contractual step-ups" (~2:12) — high-risk investment claim, blocked until Legal signs off. "World-class hospitality brand" — operator unnamed. Tambun vs Bercham — locality unconfirmed.

## Asset kit (48 assets, 144 copy variants)
Per angle × language: Reel/TikTok 15s (9:16) · Feed video 30s (1:1) · 5-card carousel (4:5) · Story static (9:16). Three copy variants (A/B/C) per asset for testing. Every end card: project, developer, *Artist's impression*, developer licence and APDL numbers.

## Budget split (share of whatever budget TKB sets)
Awareness 35% · Consideration 30% · Conversion 25% · Retargeting 10%.

## Flight
- **D1–7 Tease & learn:** all angles live at low spend; kill bottom-third hooks on day 4 by 3-second view rate.
- **D8–14 Scale winners:** 60% of awareness budget to the top two angle × language pairs; conversion ad sets to 50% viewers.
- **D15–21 Convert:** weekend gallery-visit pushes, WhatsApp-first.
- **D22–30 Close & retarget:** form openers and no-shows; leadership one-pager on cost per appointment by angle.

## Channels
Meta (main lead engine, Special Ad Category: Housing) · TikTok (hook testing, reach) · Xiaohongshu (Chinese-speaking investors, organic notes) · YouTube Shorts (reach + retargeting pool) · Google Search (brand capture once locality is confirmed).

## Speed-to-lead
Every lead gets a first WhatsApp reply in their language within 2 minutes (drafted automatically; human sends). Hot = ready within 3 months with cash or approved loan → straight to a negotiator. Appointment reminders at 24h and 2h; negotiators log no-show / visited / booking fee / SPA.

Developer licence no. · APDL no. + validity · Sales WhatsApp number · Privacy-policy URL · Master video file · Brochure + price list. See `MISSING_INPUTS.md`.
