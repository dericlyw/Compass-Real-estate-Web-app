# Meta Branded Content Rules: Compass.RealEstate / TKB

Source: Yik Chan (Facebook post, "Facebook Branded Content: 13 Categories You Should Never Promote", Nov 2026), saved by Deric on 12 Nov 2026. Check the details against Meta's Branded Content Policies before any paid partnership.

## Why this matters to us
- We are an agency (Compass) promoting a developer (TKB) for commission. Staff and agents who post FORReSsTA or Industrial Park content on their own Pages or profiles are making **commercially connected posts**. They must use Meta's **Paid Partnership** label, and every rule below applies to them.
- These rules also apply to organic posts, not only paid ones. Breaking them can mean reduced reach, limited features, removed posts or a suspended Page.

| 13 | Political / social-issue advertising | Avoid political figures and election topics in commercial posts. |

## The 8-point check before anything is posted
1. What exactly are we selling?
2. Does it fall into a restricted category?
3. Does it follow Meta's Community Standards?
4. Is the promotion legal in Malaysia? (APDL / advertising permit, Housing Development Act)
5. Does the post need a **Paid Partnership** label? (Agents, staff and KOLs: yes)
6. Does it make claims about **money, results or guarantees**?
7. Could it be seen as misleading, exaggerated or unsafe?
8. Do we have proof, permissions and clear terms for every claim?

## Banned words in our copy
guaranteed, guaranteed income / rental / return, risk-free, instant results, miracle, cure, "#1", "best value" (unless we can prove it), invented scarcity ("only X left" unless sales confirms it in writing the same day)

## Pro tips (as given)
- Read the real product page, not only the brand's pitch message.
- Get the target country, the claims and the approved messaging in writing.
- Keep screenshots of the brief, agreement, product details and approvals.
- Check the final caption, image, video, link, landing page **and comments** before publishing.
- If unsure, ask Meta support or a compliance professional before posting.
