# MVP pilot test — Urban Forest @ Bercham

**Purpose:** get structured feedback from TKB marketing, sales and management on whether this tool would cut the time from concept film to booked gallery visits — before we build the integrations.

**Duration:** 1 week. **Testers:** 5–7 people (2 marketing, 2–3 sales/negotiators, 1 management, Legal for 30 min).
**Rule:** use dummy names and phone numbers on the landing pages. Nothing is published or sent anywhere from this test.

## Before the test (Deric)
1. Enter in **Settings**: developer licence no., APDL no. + validity, sales WhatsApp number, privacy-policy URL, price list, sales roster.
   Without the permit numbers every ad stays blocked — that is correct behaviour, but testers can't review anything.
2. Share the link + access code with testers by WhatsApp.
3. Optional: **Settings → Load DEMO leads** so the inbox, appointments and report have something to show (badged DEMO).

## Tester scripts (15–20 minutes each)

### Marketing
1. **Project Intelligence** — are the claims right? Anything missing or wrong in the "unverified" list?
2. **Campaign Plan** — do the 4 angles fit how TKB sells Urban Forest? Which would you cut or add?
3. **Approval Queue** — open 3 ads in your strongest language. Edit one, approve one, reject one with a comment.
4. **Launch Kit** — download the Meta ZIP and open `ads.csv`. Could your agency upload this as is?

### Sales / negotiators
1. On your phone, open a landing page (e.g. `/lp/planned?lang=zh`) and submit a dummy enquiry.
2. **Lead Inbox** — is the drafted WhatsApp reply something you would send? Book the lead into a slot.
3. **Appointments** — is the pre-visit brief useful? Log an outcome.

### Management
1. **Overview** and **Leadership Report** — does this answer "what did we spend, what did we get, what does an SPA cost us"?
2. What would you need to see weekly to approve more budget?

### Legal (30 min)
Review the compliance rules on any ad page, the PDPA consent text on a landing page (EN/BM/中文), and the blocked 5.5% return claim.

## Feedback
Press **Feedback** (bottom-right, any page). Pick *Something's wrong / Idea / Question / Works well*. It records your name and the page.
Deric reviews everything under **Tester Feedback** and can download it as CSV.

## Questions we need answered by the end of the week
1. Would TKB marketing use this instead of briefing an agency for launch content? If not, what's missing?
2. Is the BM and Chinese copy natural enough to run with light edits?
3. Would negotiators reply within 2 minutes if the draft is ready? What stops them today?
4. Which integration matters most next: Meta lead forms, WhatsApp auto-reply, or ad upload?
5. What would management pay for this per launch / per month? (Validates the commercial model.)
