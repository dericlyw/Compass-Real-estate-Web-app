## 4. What the measurements mean (honest read)

**Where FORReSsTA wins by measurement**
1. **One full bathroom per bedroom (4/4).** Meranti has 3 baths plus a loft WC; Dahlia has 3.
2. **A 237 sf balcony.** This is a real outdoor room, which Dahlia doesn't have.
3. **Same 22' frontage as Meranti, wider than Dahlia's 18'.**
4. **A ground-floor bedroom with its own full bath.** Ready for three generations.
5. **Big bedrooms:** master 266 sf, bedroom 3 190 sf.
6. **Net internal area (1,957 sf) already exceeds Dahlia's marketed built-up (1,938 sf)**, and Dahlia's figure includes walls.

**Where Taiko wins by measurement**
1. **Meranti's lot is 5' deeper** (75' vs 70', +110 sf of land) and its house body is about **3'8" deeper**.
2. **Meranti has a loft half-storey and an upstairs family area.** FORReSsTA has a corridor instead.
3. **Meranti's marketed 2,814 sf** will look bigger than anything we publish until we state our gross built-up on the same basis.

1. **Get the official gross built-up** for Type A/A1 (intermediate), B (end) and C (corner) from Archimatrix, and update the agent pages and brochure. Drop the 1,540 sf figure.
