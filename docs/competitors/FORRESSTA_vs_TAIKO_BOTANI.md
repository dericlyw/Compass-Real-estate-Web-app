## 3. TKB FORReSsTA's advantages (the angle to sell)

1. **"A resort you live in, not a park you drive to."** Pool, gym, golf simulator, tennis, badminton, basketball and a roller-blade track are all inside your own gate. At Taiko you share public parks with a 1,200-acre township and pay per hour for pickleball.
2. **"Your backlane is a garden."** No other Ipoh terrace offers this, including Taiko's. The kids play behind the house, safely, every day.
3. **"Freehold land at the lowest price per square foot."** RM390/sf of freehold land against RM422–442/sf at Botani. Land appreciates; bricks depreciate.
4. **"Walk in with almost no cash."** The developer absorbs the down payment, legal fees, stamp duty and disbursement (subject to bank margin). A Taiko buyer needs **about RM62k–91k** in cash on day one.
5. **"Every bedroom gets its own bathroom."** 4 bed / 4 bath against 3 bath at Taiko.
6. **"A forest that is yours."** Five named neighbourhoods, one gate and 787 families, against a phase number in a big township.
7. **"Closer to town."** 5.6 km to AEON Station 18 and 11 km to Ipoh town; Taiko quotes 12 km to the city.
8. **"Brand new, built to 2028 standards"**, against designs approved for 2023–2024.

## 4. Objection handling (when buyers raise Taiko)

| Buyer says | Reply |
|---|---|
| "Meranti is 2,800 sq ft for RM729k." | "That's a 2.5-storey with a loft, so you're paying for stairs and an attic. Here you get freehold land at RM390/sf against RM442/sf there, your own pool, gym and stadium, and almost no cash to get in. Work out what you'd spend in a year on a gym, a pool and courts for the family." |
| "Taiko is ready now; yours is 2028." | "Buying off-plan means you pay progressively, and the package covers your entry costs. Your money isn't all locked in on day one the way a completed unit's 10% plus fees is. And you choose your forest and lot while the best ones are open." |
| "Botani has the hospital and international school." | "They're about 8 km away, roughly a 10-minute drive. You get the same hospital and school, plus AEON Station 18 and Ipoh town closer. Then come home to a quiet gated forest." |
| "RM300 a month maintenance?" | "That's RM10 a day for a pool, gym, golf simulator, tennis, basketball, roller rink, guards and landscaping. One family gym membership costs more." |
| "Dahlia is only RM494k." | "It's leasehold, on an 18-foot lot, with 3 bathrooms. Ours is freehold, on a 22-foot lot, with 4 bathrooms and a clubhouse. The gap at resale is bigger than the gap in price." |
| "Strata title?" | "That's normal for every gated scheme with shared facilities. It's what protects the gate, the pool and the gardens. You still own your house freehold." |
